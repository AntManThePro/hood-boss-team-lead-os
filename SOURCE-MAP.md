# Hood Boss Team Lead OS — Source Map

## Primary source packet
11-page uploaded Team Leads PDF packet.

### Pages 1–2 — Exhibit A: Hood Boss, LLC Team Lead Job Description
Older/formal role description and technician requirements. Retained as reference rather than silently merged with the later Hood Boss-branded description.

### Pages 3–4 — Team Lead Interview Questions
Structured interview in four areas:
- Experience
- Performance
- Communication Evaluation
- General Questions

The app preserves the source questions and probes as the interview script.

### Page 5 — Workflow for Work Orders [TECHNICIANS]
Operational state flow:
On the Way → On Site → Waiting on Access → Check In → Check Out → Needs SM Review

### Page 6 — Hood, Duct, and Grease Fan Deep Cleaning Instructions
Technical reference page. The v1 app indexes it in the source binder but does not rewrite it into new company policy.

### Pages 7–11 — Hood Boss Team Lead Job Description
Later Hood Boss-branded operational description, including:
- Team Lead accountability at customer sites
- pre-job review and preparation
- MyTechPix clock-in
- job outcome documentation
- accessible-area cleaning/documentation
- safety correction/escalation
- Control Tower communication
- Mid Job Check-In / CT Job Approval statuses
- final inspection and Team Lead responsibility
- weekly inventory and vehicle cleanliness
- monthly pressure-washer oil changes
- professionalism
- qualification gates
- communication expectations
- decision making
- coaching and team development
- physical/additional requirements

## Source handling rule
This build does not silently reconcile conflicting or overlapping language between the two job-description versions. The later Hood Boss-branded pages drive the v1 readiness workflow; the earlier Exhibit A version remains visible as reference.
